# Material and price comparison evidence

`snippets/price-comparison.liquid` compares a MUNLER mirror against typical UK retail.
Under the Digital Markets, Competition and Consumers Act 2024 and the CAP Code rules on
comparative advertising, a comparison must be objective, must compare products meeting the
same need, must not denigrate, and must be **evidenced on the date it was made**. This file
is that evidence.

**Re-verify before every theme release and update `checked_on` in the snippet.** If the
evidence stops supporting a row, delete the row rather than leave a stale claim up.

We deliberately do **not name any competitor**. Every claim is at the level of "what UK
retailers in this price band publish", which is what the evidence below supports.

---

60 to 100 cm tall, in the £120 to £400 band. Checked **2026-07-10**.

## Tally across roughly 35 products

- **Stainless steel: 1.** A third-party Amazon bathroom mirror ("SUS304"), quoted from the
  listing bullets, not a mainstream UK retailer, and not page-verified at spec level.
- **Iron: about 9.** Dunelm, Atkin & Thyme, La Redoute, Oliver Bonas ×4, Wayfair ×2.
- **Wood: about 9.** Pine, elm, rubberwood, eucalyptus, mango, generic.
- **MDF stated as the frame itself: 3.** Plus MDF as backing on most iron and wood pieces.
- **Aluminium: 2.** One of which is an arched, not asymmetrical, mirror.
- **"Metal", type unspecified: about 7.**
- **Frameless: 3. Unstated: 3.**

**No stainless steel was found at any mainstream UK homeware or design retailer in this band.**

## The claim we are entitled to make

> At £120 to £400, from high-street retailers (Dunelm, John Lewis, Oliver Bonas, La Redoute,
> Cox & Cox, Atkin & Thyme, Graham & Green) to designer direct-to-consumer brands (Fableroom,
> Blossholm, William Wood), asymmetrical mirrors are framed in **wood, iron, MDF, moulded PU
> or resin, or plain "metal" whose grade the seller does not state**. We found **none framed
> in stainless steel**.

That sentence is quote-backed by the two tables in this file.

The unconditionally safe core, which survived both rounds of research, is:
**"we found none using stainless steel"**.

### Wording rules

- Say **iron, wood or MDF**. Do **not** say aluminium is typical: only two examples, and one
  of them was not an asymmetrical mirror.
- Say **"we found no mainstream UK retailer using stainless steel"**, not "nobody uses
  stainless steel". Several pages say only "Metal" or state nothing; we can prove they do
  not *claim* stainless, not that they are not stainless.
- Do **not** say their mirrors are badly made, will rust, or will fail. Iron and wood are
  legitimate materials. The claim is about what you get for the money, not about defects.
- Do **not** name a retailer on the site.

### Why we write "metal" in quotation marks

About fifteen of these mirrors state only "Metal Frame" with no grade. It is a reasonable
inference that a brand using stainless steel would say so, since it is a selling point. **That
inference is not evidence.** So the table quotes their word and states ours, and lets the
reader draw the conclusion. Never write on the site that their metal *is* iron.

### Rows we removed because they stopped being true

- **UK delivery.** We used to claim theirs is "often charged". **William Wood gives free UK
  delivery** and Blossholm ships free within Europe. It is no longer a difference. Row deleted.
- **Ships from.** Deleted with the Ethnik Living comparison.

### ⚠️ The warranty row, and what it does not say

**Blossholm offers a 10-year warranty. That is twice ours, and they beat us on this.** They also
offer a 100-day try-at-home. We do not say so on the site because the column is scoped to UK
sellers, and Blossholm is Danish.

Within UK sellers the row is true: **William Wood states 1 year**, Fableroom offers a 365-day
returns guarantee rather than a warranty, and no high-street retailer states any warranty at all.

This scoping is defensible but it is convenient, and it was a deliberate choice. **If anyone
widens that row beyond the UK, or if a UK seller starts offering more than five years, the row
must be deleted or MUNLER must raise its warranty.** Do not let it quietly go stale.

## Two genuine white-spaces

- **Copper-free / lead-free glass.** Found exactly once across the whole set, on an £89.82
  budget Amazon listing ("4MM copper-free silver mirror"). **No lead-free claim anywhere.**
  Neither the high-street retailers nor the designer brands (Fableroom, Blossholm, William
  Wood) publish a copper-free or lead-free glass claim. This is the single cleanest claim
  MUNLER owns, and it survived both rounds of research.
  - Note: **William Wood does publish glass thickness** ("4mm silvered mirror glass") on most
    spec tables. So the glass row must not say they state nothing about glass. It says only
    that no copper-free or lead-free claim was found, which is exactly what we verified.
- **Warranty.** Among **UK sellers**, no multi-year warranty beyond William Wood's 1 year.
  Retailers state none at all. **But see the warranty warning above: Blossholm, Danish, offers
  10 years.**

## Price bands

- **Wall mirrors, 60 to 100 cm, non-stainless, page-verified in band:** core cluster
  **£130 to £250** (La Redoute £120.99, Graham & Green £129, M&S £139, La Redoute £149.99,
  Oliver Bonas £160, Graham & Green £165, Oliver Bonas £195, Atkin & Thyme £219,
  John Lewis £250). Premium tail at Cox & Cox £425.
  - MUNLER Brook is **£199 (73×52 cm)** and **£259 (90×65 cm)**: inside the same band.
  - So the honest headline for Brook is **"same money, better material"**, not "cheaper".
  - The module shows **a band per size**, because the small-mirror band would misrepresent
    the larger one. 73×52 cm is compared against **£130 to £250**; 90×65 cm against
    **£195 to £425** (Oliver Bonas £195 at 81×77, Atkin & Thyme £219 at 90×90,
    John Lewis £250 at 115×65, Cox & Cox £425 at 150×80).

- **Designer direct-to-consumer brands** run higher than the high street: Fableroom £102 to
  £258, Blossholm £219 to £449, William Wood £69 to £549 with the bulk of full-length shaped
  pieces at £199 to £449. **Combined roughly £100 to £550, concentrated at £200 to £400.**
  Brook at £199 and £259 sits inside this too, and below most of it.

- **Full-length mirrors:** far thinner evidence. Cox & Cox recycled elm 150×80 at **£425**;
  West Elm eucalyptus 167×72 at **£569**. MUNLER Solenne is **£749 at 170×90 cm**.
  - **Solenne is more expensive than both.** We therefore do **not** show a price row on the
    Solenne page. Showing one would either mislead or lose the argument. The Solenne module
    compares materials, glass and warranty only.
  - If a price row is ever added for Solenne, it must state that we are more expensive and
    say why. Do not quietly omit a bad number while implying a favourable one.
